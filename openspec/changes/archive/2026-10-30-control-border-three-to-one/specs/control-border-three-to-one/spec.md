# control-border-three-to-one Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A control border is 3:1 on every ground it sits on

In the Zuiddrecht-type sets the control border (`--nldesign-color-border-dark`) SHALL be at least 3:1 against white, the
page ground, the table head, the bulk band and the switch ground (WCAG 1.4.11).

#### Scenario: A board button on the page ground

- **GIVEN** the Zuiddrecht set and the board look
- **THEN** a secondary header button's border is #828A95, 3.23:1 against #F5F6F8
