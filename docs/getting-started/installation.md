# Installing Thematiq

Installing Thematiq takes about one minute. You do it from inside Nextcloud — no server access needed.

## Requirements

- Nextcloud 32 or newer
- An administrator account

## Step 1 — Open the Apps section

Log in to Nextcloud as an administrator. Click your **avatar** (top-right corner) and choose **Apps**.

## Step 2 — Search for Thematiq

On the Apps page, type **Thematiq** in the search field. The app is listed in the **Customization** category.

You can also look it up on the [Nextcloud App Store](https://apps.nextcloud.com/apps/thematiq) website.

## Step 3 — Click "Download and enable"

Click **Download and enable** next to Thematiq. Nextcloud downloads the app and turns it on.

:::note
Installing Thematiq does not change how your Nextcloud looks. It starts with the standard Nextcloud theme, and nothing changes until you choose a theme yourself.
:::

## Step 4 — Open the Thematiq settings

1. Click your **avatar** (top-right corner)
2. Choose **Administration settings**
3. Click **Theming** in the left sidebar, under **Administration**
4. Scroll down past Nextcloud's own theming settings (name, colours, logo) until you reach the heading **Thematiq**

You are in the right place when you see the **Thematiq** heading with a **Documentation** link next to it, and a **Design token set** list below it.

:::tip
If your Nextcloud is set to another language, the menu items are translated too. In Dutch, for example, **Theming** is called **Uiterlijk**.
:::

→ Continue to [Choose your theme](./configuration.md) to pick your organisation's theme.

---

## Removing the app

To undo everything Thematiq changed, click **Reset theme to Nextcloud** in the Thematiq settings first. This also puts Nextcloud's own theming colours and logo back to their defaults.

Then go to **Apps**, find Thematiq and click **Disable**, then **Remove**.

If you remove the app without resetting first, the Thematiq styling disappears, but the colours and logo it copied into Nextcloud's own theming settings stay until you change them under **Theming**. See [Theming sync](../features/theming-sync.md).
