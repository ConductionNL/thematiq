# Thematiq — User Guide

**Thematiq** is a Nextcloud app that gives your Nextcloud the look and feel of your organisation: your colours, fonts and logo on every screen, edited live and without writing code.

You can start from a ready-made theme or build your own:

- **Ready-made themes** — more than 60 themes are included, among them the Dutch national government (Rijkshuisstijl), many municipalities and provinces, and neutral examples for schools and colleges. See the full list under [Token sets](./reference/token-sets/index.md).
- **Your own brand identity** — not on the list? Create a theme from two colours and a logo, upload your organisation's design tokens, or install one from the [theme gallery](./features/theme-gallery.md). See [Custom token sets](./features/custom-token-sets.md).

## What you can do with it

- **Apply your organisation's style guide** — pick a theme from the list, or one you created yourself
- **Check it before anyone else sees it** — the preview shows the result straight away, and you can try a theme in your own session first
- **Fine-tune individual colours** — adjust specific colours in the token editor without touching any code
- **Export and import your settings** — download your customisations as a file and use them on another Nextcloud

## Who should read this guide

This guide is for **Nextcloud administrators** who want to apply their organisation's branding. You don't need any technical knowledge — if you can log in to Nextcloud's administration settings, you can use Thematiq.

## Where to start

1. **[Install the app](./getting-started/installation.md)** — takes about a minute via the Nextcloud App Store
2. **[Choose your theme](./getting-started/configuration.md)** — pick a theme from the list and apply it
3. **[Fine-tune if needed](./features/token-editor.md)** — optionally adjust individual colours in the token editor
